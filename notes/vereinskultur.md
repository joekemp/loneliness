The German contrast with the UK is stark: Vereinskultur — hundreds of thousands of registered clubs where recurring, unforced, obligation-free encounter is built into civic life. Britain had the working men's club, the chapel, the union branch, the youth club. Those eroded far more completely, and the erosion is recent, political, and datable (austerity after 2010 gutted libraries, Sure Start, youth services, community centres; roughly a quarter of pubs have closed since 2000). So the reserve is old, but the thinning of the counterweight is new.
